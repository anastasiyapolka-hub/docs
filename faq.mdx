CoTel is an AI copilot for Telegram that lets you analyze chats, channels, and groups without leaving your browser. Whether you are just getting started or trying to understand a specific feature, the answers below cover the most common questions.

## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is CoTel?">
    CoTel is a web-based AI assistant for Telegram. It connects to your Telegram account (or uses a shared service account for public chats) and lets you run AI-powered analysis on any chat, channel, or group. You can summarize discussions, search for specific topics, extract keywords, and set up automated subscriptions that deliver periodic digests or alerts directly to your Telegram.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is CoTel free to use?">
    Yes. CoTel offers a Free plan at no cost that includes 3 requests per day (60 per month), up to 7 days of chat history analysis, and 2 trial subscriptions. Paid plans — Basic ($9/mo) and Pro ($24/mo) — unlock higher request limits, longer history windows, more active subscriptions, and shorter subscription intervals.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What languages does CoTel support?">
    The CoTel interface is available in **English** and **Russian**. You can switch languages at any time by clicking the globe icon in the navigation bar.

    Analysis itself is not limited to these two languages. Because the underlying AI models understand natural language broadly, CoTel can analyze chat content written in most languages — you write your query in the language you prefer and the AI handles the rest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What AI models does CoTel use?">
    CoTel currently supports two models, which you can choose per-query in the Query settings panel (sidebar → Chat requests → Query settings → AI model) or set a default in your profile (Profile → Profile settings → Default AI model):

    - **OpenAI GPT-4.1 mini** — used for fast, everyday analysis. This is the default model on the Free tier.
    - **Claude Sonnet 4.6** — better suited for deeper analysis of long discussions with a large amount of context.

    Free accounts always use OpenAI for subscriptions regardless of the setting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy & data

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does CoTel store my Telegram messages?">
    No. Analysis is performed on-the-fly: CoTel reads the relevant messages from Telegram at the moment you run a query and passes them to the AI model. CoTel does not build or retain a copy of your message history. The app itself notes: "We don't store message history."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my Telegram password safe?">
    CoTel uses Telegram's official authentication flow. Your Telegram password is only used during the sign-in step to verify your identity with Telegram directly. It is never stored by CoTel. If you use QR-code login, no password is transmitted to CoTel's servers at all.

    For accounts with Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) enabled, your Telegram password is encrypted in the browser before being sent and is used only to complete the Telegram session handshake.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should I upload confidential data?">
    No. CoTel displays the following disclaimer on the analysis dashboard:

    > "Chat analysis is performed by a third-party AI model (OpenAI). Do not upload secret or confidential data."

    Messages you submit for analysis are processed by OpenAI's API. Avoid running analysis on chats that contain trade secrets, personal identification information, or any other sensitive data you would not want processed by a third-party AI provider.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data when I delete my account?">
    Deleting your CoTel profile permanently removes all data associated with your account: your active Telegram session, saved chat history, all subscriptions, and your profile working data. This action cannot be undone. To delete your account, go to Profile → Profile settings → Delete profile and follow the confirmation steps.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Analysis

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What analysis types are available?">
    CoTel supports four main analysis modes that you select when writing your query:

    - **Summarization** — produces a concise summary of the conversation over the selected time period.
    - **Notes / Digest** — extracts key points, decisions, or highlights into a structured digest format.
    - **Search** — finds messages or threads that match a specific topic, keyword, or question.
    - **Keywords** — identifies the most prominent topics and terms discussed in the chat.

    You describe what you want in plain language; the AI model interprets your query and applies the appropriate approach.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How far back can CoTel analyze my chat history?">
    The available history window depends on your plan:

    | Plan  | History analysis period |
    |-------|------------------------|
    | Free  | Up to 7 days           |
    | Basic | Up to 30 days          |
    | Pro   | Up to 90 days          |

    You control how many days to look back in **Query settings** (sidebar → Chat requests → Query settings → History analysis period, days). If you set a value that exceeds your plan's limit, CoTel will show an error and prompt you to reduce it or upgrade.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I analyze private chats?">
    Yes, but only if you connect your personal Telegram account. Private chats, private groups, and private channels are not accessible through the CoTel service account, which is limited to public chats and channels.

    To connect your personal account, use the **Connect to Telegram** section in the sidebar and sign in via QR code or phone number.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file format can I import for analysis?">
    CoTel accepts **Telegram export JSON files** — the format produced by Telegram Desktop's built-in export feature (Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data). Select "Telegram file (JSON)" as the data source in the sidebar, then import the file.

    Note that Telegram's HTML export format is not supported; make sure to export as JSON.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Subscriptions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a subscription?">
    A subscription is a recurring AI analysis job. You define a chat or channel to monitor, a query describing what to look for (for example: "new product announcements" or "weekly digest of discussions"), and how often CoTel should check for new messages. CoTel runs the analysis automatically at the chosen interval and sends the results to your Telegram account via the CoTel bot.

    There are two subscription types:
    - **Events** — reacts to messages matching your query and sends a list of specific matching messages.
    - **Digest** — prepares an AI summary of all new messages for the given period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get notified?">
    CoTel delivers subscription results directly to your Telegram account through the CoTel bot. To receive notifications you must start the bot at least once: in the Subscriptions panel, click **Launch bot**. After that, every time a subscription run finds a match (Events) or completes a digest (Digest), the result is sent as a message from the CoTel bot in your Telegram app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do trial subscriptions work?">
    The Free plan includes up to 2 trial subscriptions. Trial subscriptions work the same as regular subscriptions but expire automatically after their trial period ends — you cannot delete them manually before they expire. Once both trial subscriptions have expired, the Free plan no longer allows creating new subscriptions. Upgrade to the Basic or Pro plan to create permanent, non-expiring subscriptions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I subscribe to private chats?">
    Subscribing to private chats, private groups, and private channels is only available when you are signed in with your personal Telegram account. The CoTel service account can only monitor public chats and channels. You will see a note in the subscription creation form if you attempt to select a private chat without a personal account connected.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I change my plan?">
    Open your profile by clicking your avatar or name in the top navigation, then click **Change plan**. Select the plan you want and confirm. The change takes effect immediately and your new limits are shown in the Profile → Limits tab.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change the interface language?">
    Click the **globe icon** in the navigation bar. This toggles the interface between English and Russian. The setting is applied immediately without requiring a page reload.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I forget my CoTel password?">
    On the login page, click **Forgot your password?** below the password field. Enter your email address and CoTel will send a password reset code to that address. Enter the code on the next screen along with your new password to complete the reset.

    If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder. You can request a new code using the **Resend code** button after the cooldown period expires.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
