## How these documents relate to each other

The Terms of Service is the main document governing the use of CoTel. The other documents supplement it and explain specific topics in more detail.

The Privacy Policy governs data processing. The Cookie Policy clarifies the use of cookies and local storage. The Sub-processors List shows which external providers may participate in the operation of the Service. The Consent to Personal Data Processing records the User’s consent to data processing where applicable.

The AI Disclaimer explains the limitations of artificial intelligence and analysis results. The Acceptable Use Policy sets the rules for acceptable use of CoTel. The Billing and Subscription Policy governs payments, subscriptions, and refunds. Operator Details contains information about the Operator and official communication channels.
