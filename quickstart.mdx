---
title: "Quickstart: run your first CoTel Telegram analysis"
description: "Follow these steps to create an account, connect Telegram, and run your first AI analysis of a chat or channel in under five minutes."
---

CoTel is ready to use as soon as you connect a Telegram source. This guide walks you through every step — from creating your account to reading your first AI-generated result. The whole process takes under five minutes.

<Tip>
  The Free plan includes 3 requests per day — more than enough to evaluate CoTel before upgrading. No credit card is required.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your CoTel account">
    Open CoTel and click **Sign in / Sign up**. Enter your email address and click **Continue**, then select **Sign up** on the next screen.

    Fill in the registration form:

    - **Email** — the address where your verification code will be sent
    - **Country** — selected once at registration and used to localize settings
    - **Time zone** — affects the displayed time in CoTel bot notifications for subscriptions
    - **Password** — must be at least 8 characters
    
    Check the box confirming you have read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service and are at least 16 years old, then click **Sign up**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    CoTel sends a 6-digit verification code to the email address you provided. Enter the code in the verification screen and click **Verify**. If the code doesn't arrive within a minute, click **Resend code**.

    Once verified, you are signed in automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in to your account">
    On future visits, open CoTel and click **Sign in / Sign up**. Enter your email address, click **Continue**, then enter your password and click **Sign in**.

    If you forget your password, click **Forgot your password?** to request a reset code by email.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Telegram">
    In the left sidebar of the work area, expand the **Connect to Telegram** section. Choose one of two connection methods:

    - **My Telegram account** — CoTel authenticates as your personal Telegram account. You can analyze private chats, groups, and channels your account has access to. Enter your phone number and confirm the code sent to your Telegram app. If your account has two-factor authentication enabled, you will also be prompted for your Telegram password.
    - **CoTel service account** — CoTel uses its own Telegram account to read publicly accessible channels. No personal login is required.

    After a successful connection, your username appears in the sidebar with a green status indicator.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select a chat or channel">
    Once connected, the **Chat requests** section of the sidebar shows your available chats and channels. Click a chat from the list, or type a name or link directly into the chat input field.

    You can also configure **Query settings** (expand that section) to adjust the analysis history period in days and the AI model to use for this request.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose an analysis type and enter your prompt">
    In the main input area at the bottom of the screen, type your request. The analysis type is determined by what you ask:

    - **Summarization** — ask for a summary or recap of messages
    - **Notes / Digest** — ask for structured notes or a topic breakdown
    - **Search** — ask a specific question to find relevant messages
    - **Keywords** — ask to extract key terms, names, or entities

    You can also pick a ready-made template from the templates section to pre-fill a well-formed prompt.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run the analysis and view results">
    Click the **play** button (or press Enter) to start the analysis. A loading indicator appears while CoTel fetches messages and sends them to the AI model. Processing typically takes a few seconds to a couple of minutes depending on the volume of messages.

    Results appear in the main panel as formatted text.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export or save the result">
    After the analysis completes, you can:

    - **Copy** the result to your clipboard
    - **Export** it to a portable format
    - **Repeat** the same analysis later from the History section
    
    All past analyses are saved to your history and accessible from the **History** navigation item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Chat analysis is performed by a third-party AI model (OpenAI). Do not upload secret or confidential data.
</Note>
