AI may confidently refer to non-existent sources, facts, quotations, dates, or names. The User must take this feature into account and must not rely on the Results as an independent source of truth.

The Service analyzes messages and materials from Telegram chats and channels. In this regard, the following limitations are possible:

1. the data sample may be incomplete or fragmentary, for example if a message has been deleted, edited, or is unavailable through the Telegram API;

4. messages may be analyzed outside their full historical context;
