## How to sign in with a QR code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Connect to Telegram section">
    In the CoTel workspace, open the **Connect to Telegram** section in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select My Telegram account">
    Select **My Telegram account** as the data source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the QR sign-in section">
    The **Sign in via QR code** section appears — click **Get QR code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the QR code">
    CoTel generates a QR code and displays it on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Link Desktop Device on your phone">
    On your phone, open Telegram, then go to **Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Scan the QR code displayed in CoTel with your phone camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the login">
    Confirm the login in the Telegram app when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your 2FA password if required">
    If you have Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) enabled, CoTel will prompt you to enter your Telegram password to complete sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## QR code expiry

QR codes expire after a short time. If the code expires before you scan it, click **Refresh QR** to generate a new one. The status message will read "The QR code has expired. Refresh the QR." when this happens.

## When QR login is preferable

- You prefer not to type your phone number into a web app
- You want faster sign-in without waiting for an SMS or in-app message code
