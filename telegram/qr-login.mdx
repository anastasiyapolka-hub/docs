## How to sign in via QR code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Telegram connection section">
    In the CoTel workspace, open the **Connect to Telegram** section in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select My Telegram account">
    Select **My Telegram account** as the data source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the QR code sign-in section">
    The **QR code sign-in** section will appear — click **Get QR code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the QR code">
    CoTel will generate a QR code and display it on the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Link Desktop Device on your phone">
    On your phone, open Telegram, then go to **Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Scan the QR code shown in CoTel with your phone camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm sign-in">
    Confirm the sign-in in the Telegram app when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your 2FA password if required">
    If you have two-factor authentication (2FA) enabled, CoTel will ask you to enter your Telegram password to complete sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## QR code expiration

QR codes are valid for a limited time. If the code expires before you scan it, click **Refresh QR code** to generate a new one. In this case, the status message will look like this: “The QR code has expired. Refresh the QR code.”

## When QR code sign-in is more convenient

- You do not want to enter your phone number in the web app
- You want to sign in faster without waiting for an SMS or a code in the Telegram app
