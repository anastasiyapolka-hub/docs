## Why connect your Telegram account?

Connecting your personal Telegram account allows CoTel to:

- Analyze your private chats, groups, and channels that you are a member of
- Access historical messages for the period allowed by your plan
- Create subscriptions on private chats

<Warning>
  CoTel does not store your message history. Analysis is performed on-the-fly using data retrieved from Telegram during each request.
</Warning>

## Two connection methods

- **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, receive a code in Telegram, then confirm the code. If Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is enabled, CoTel will also prompt you to enter your Telegram password.
- **QR code**: Faster sign-in — generate a QR code and scan it using the Telegram mobile app (**Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device**).

## Data source options in the workspace

The **Connect to Telegram** section in the sidebar offers three modes:

1. **My Telegram account** — Uses your personal Telegram account (requires phone or QR authentication).
2. **CoTel service account** — Uses CoTel's shared service account (no personal authentication needed; public chats and channels only).
3. **Telegram file (JSON)** — Upload a Telegram export JSON file for offline analysis (advanced).

<Note>
  Subscribing to private and personal chats requires signing in with your personal Telegram account. The CoTel service account only accesses public chats and channels.
</Note>

## Session management

- Your Telegram session is securely maintained by CoTel.
- To end the session, click **End session** in the Telegram status panel in the sidebar.
- With **Enhanced security** enabled in your profile settings, signing out of CoTel also ends your Telegram session automatically.
