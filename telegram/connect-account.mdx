## Why connect a Telegram account?

Connecting your personal Telegram account allows CoTel to:

- Analyze your public and private chats, groups, and channels that you belong to
- Access message history for the period available on your plan
- Create subscriptions for public and private chats, groups, and channels

<Warning>
  CoTel does not store your message history. History analysis is performed on the fly using data retrieved from Telegram during each request.
</Warning>

## Two connection methods

- **Phone number**: enter your phone number, receive a code in Telegram, and confirm it. If two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled, CoTel will also ask you to enter your Telegram password.
- **QR code**: a faster sign-in method — generate a QR code and scan it in the Telegram mobile app (**Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device**). Two-factor authentication will also be required if enabled.

<Warning>
  CoTel does not store your Telegram two-factor authentication password. If Telegram requests a 2FA password, it is encrypted directly in the browser before being sent to the server using a one-time 2048-bit RSA key and the OAEP / SHA-256 scheme. On the server, the password is decrypted only in runtime memory, used once to authorize Telegram, and immediately deleted.
</Warning>

## CoTel connection modes for Telegram

The **Connect to Telegram** section in the sidebar offers two modes:

1. **My Telegram account** — uses your personal Telegram account and requires authorization by phone number or QR code.
2. **CoTel service account** — uses a CoTel service account, does not require personal authorization, and is available only for public channels and groups.

<Note>
  Subscriptions to private and personal chats require signing in through your personal Telegram account. The CoTel service account can access only public channels and groups.
</Note>

## Managing your Telegram session

- Your Telegram session is stored in CoTel in encrypted form using Fernet / AES-128. It is used only to perform actions that you initiate yourself: analysis of selected chats, as well as the subscriptions you create.
- To end the session, click **End session** in the Telegram status panel in the sidebar.
- If **Enhanced security** is enabled in your profile settings, signing out of CoTel also automatically ends your Telegram session.
- When you delete your account, all saved sessions are deleted.
