## What is a CoTel service account?

A CoTel service account is a shared Telegram account managed by CoTel. When you choose this data source, CoTel uses its own Telegram credentials to read public chats and channels on its behalf — personal Telegram authorization is not required. It is designed for scenarios where the user does not need access to personal or private chats, but wants to quickly analyze an open channel, public group, or chat by link.

## What you can analyze

- Public Telegram channels
- Public groups
- Any chat available through a public username or link

## Limitations compared to a personal account

- No access to private chats or groups you belong to
- No access to chats that require membership to read
- Telegram limits apply to shared service accounts, so flood wait delays may occasionally occur
- If reading a channel requires joining it first, the service account may not be able to join automatically

<Info>
  For better support of private chats and personalized subscriptions, connect your own Telegram account.
</Info>

## Benefits of service mode

- **No need to connect your personal Telegram account.**\
  You can work with public sources without entering your phone number and without creating a personal Telegram session.
- **A quick way to test CoTel.**\
  Service mode is suitable for getting familiar with the service and checking the main analysis features.
- **Good for one-time checks of open sources.**\
  Convenient when you need to quickly analyze a public channel, chat, or group by link.
- **Convenient for users who are not ready for personal authorization yet.**\
  You can start with public sources and connect your personal Telegram account later if you need access to private chats.

## Possible error messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "This chat is not available for reading under the service account. Connect your personal Telegram or choose a public chat." | The chat is private or requires membership. Switch to **My Telegram account** or choose a public chat. |
| "Chat or channel not found. Check the link, username or chat id." | The link, username, or chat ID you entered could not be found. Check the data and try again. |
| "The invite link is invalid or has expired." | The invite link no longer works. Request a new link from the chat administrator. |
| "A join request has been sent for this chat. It will become available only after approval." | The channel requires administrator approval before the service account can join. Wait for approval or use a public chat. |
| "Service accounts have temporarily reached the Telegram limit for joining channels. Please try again later." | The service account has reached Telegram’s temporary channel-joining limit. Wait a few minutes and try again. |
| "Telegram has temporarily limited access for the service account. Please try again later." | A flood wait limit is active. Wait and try again later. |
