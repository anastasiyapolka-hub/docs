The CoTel service account is a convenient way to start analyzing public Telegram content without any personal authentication. Select it as your data source, and CoTel handles the Telegram connection on your behalf.

## What is the CoTel service account?

The CoTel service account is a shared Telegram account managed by CoTel. When you select this data source, CoTel uses its own Telegram credentials to read public chats and channels on your behalf — no personal Telegram authentication is required.

## What you can analyze

- Public Telegram channels
- Public groups
- Any chat accessible via a public username or link

## Limitations compared to a personal account

- Cannot access private chats or groups you are a member of
- Cannot access chats that require membership to read
- Subject to Telegram's rate limits for shared service accounts, which may occasionally result in flood wait delays
- If a channel requires joining before reading, the service account may not be able to join automatically

<Info>
  For the best experience with private chats and personalized subscriptions, connect your own Telegram account.
</Info>

## Error messages you may see

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "This chat is not available for reading under the service account. Connect your personal Telegram or choose a public chat." | The chat is private or requires membership. Switch to **My Telegram account** or choose a public chat. |
| "Chat or channel not found. Check the link, username or chat id." | The link, username, or chat ID you entered could not be resolved. Double-check and try again. |
| "The invite link is invalid or has expired." | The invite link no longer works. Request a fresh invite link from the chat admin. |
| "A join request has been sent for this chat. It will become available only after approval." | The channel requires admin approval before the service account can join. Wait for approval or use a public chat instead. |
| "Service accounts have temporarily reached the Telegram limit for joining channels. Please try again later." | Telegram's rate limit for the service account has been reached. Wait a few minutes and retry. |
| "Telegram has temporarily limited access for the service account. Please try again later." | A flood wait is in effect. Wait and retry shortly. |
