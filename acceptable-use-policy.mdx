## 1. General provisions

This Acceptable Use Policy (hereinafter — the “Policy”, “AUP”) defines acceptable and prohibited ways of using the CoTel web service (hereinafter — the “Service”) and sets out the consequences of violating them.

By using the Service, the User confirms that they have read this Policy and undertake to comply with it. This Policy forms an integral part of the Terms of Service and applies together with the Privacy Policy, AI Disclaimer, Cookie Policy, and Billing Policy.

In the event of a conflict between this Policy and other Service documents with respect to acceptable use, the provisions of this Policy shall apply.

## 2. General requirements

When using the Service, the User undertakes to:

1. use the Service only for lawful purposes and in accordance with applicable law;
2. respect the rights and legitimate interests of third parties, including rights to privacy, personal data protection, and intellectual property;
3. act in good faith, not abuse the provided functionality, and not use the Service in ways not intended by its purpose;
4. comply with applicable sanctions and export control regimes, including restrictions imposed by regulators in the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom, and other jurisdictions, as well as other special restrictions applicable to the use of AI services;
5. comply with the terms of use of third-party services through which the Service operates, in particular Telegram.

## 3. Working with data and Telegram

When using the Service to analyze Telegram chats and channels, the User undertakes to:

1. use the Service only to analyze data to which the User has lawful access, whether as a chat participant, channel owner, or in relation to publicly available channels;
2. not attempt to obtain access through the Service to chats, channels, or messages to which the User does not have the relevant rights, including through impersonation, circumvention of privacy restrictions, or social engineering;
3. not use the Service for mass unauthorized data collection (data scraping), creation of shadow archives of closed communities, or other actions that violate Telegram rules or personal data protection laws;
4. not use the Service to circumvent Telegram restrictions imposed on the User or on the analyzed sources.

### 3.1. Legal basis for content analysis

The User confirms that they have an appropriate legal basis for analyzing content transmitted to the Service or accessed by the Service at the User’s initiative. Such basis may include, in particular:

1. consent of chat participants to the analysis of their messages;
2. the User’s legitimate interest in relation to content that directly concerns them, for example their own messages and correspondence;
3. the public nature of the source, including open Telegram channels and public groups;
4. other grounds expressly provided by applicable personal data protection and secrecy of communications laws.

CoTel does not verify whether the User has rights and grounds to analyze specific data and is not responsible for their absence. The User undertakes to independently ensure the lawfulness of their use of the Service in each specific case and releases the Operator from any third-party claims related to the analysis of their data through the Service, in the manner provided in the “Indemnification by the User” section of the Terms of Service.

## 4. Prohibited actions

### 4.1. Illegal use

It is prohibited to use the Service:

1. for activities that violate applicable law, including criminal, administrative, civil, or tax law;
2. to circumvent restrictions of Telegram, AI providers, payment providers, or other third parties;
3. in violation of applicable sanctions and export control regimes;
4. to assist third parties in committing offenses.

### 4.2. Violation of third-party rights

It is prohibited to use the Service:

1. to violate the privacy of other users, including unauthorized disclosure of other people’s private messages;
2. to distribute third-party personal data without an appropriate legal basis (doxxing);
3. for harassment, threats, insults, blackmail, or other forms of harmful impact on individuals or legal entities;
4. to infringe intellectual property rights, including copyright and related rights, trademark rights, and rights to trade names;
5. to harm the business reputation of third parties by disseminating knowingly false or factually distorted information.

### 4.3. Abuse of functionality

It is prohibited to:

1. create excessive load on the Service, including through automated means not provided by the Service itself;
2. attempt to circumvent Plan limits, anti-fraud mechanisms, and other technical or organizational restrictions of the Service;
3. create duplicate or false Accounts, including for the purpose of circumventing restrictions or sanctions;
4. use the Service in ways not provided by its functionality and official documentation.

### 4.4. Manipulation and misleading use

It is prohibited to:

1. use the Service to create, amplify, or mass-distribute knowingly false or misleading information, fake news, or disinformation campaigns;
2. present AI Results as guaranteedly accurate, neutral, or official; when publicly using Results, it is recommended to explicitly state that they were obtained using AI and may contain errors;
3. present Results obtained using the Service as work of third parties or experts to whom they do not relate.

### 4.5. Interference with the Service

It is prohibited to:

1. attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Service, its infrastructure, other Users’ accounts, source code, or internal data;
2. perform reverse engineering, disassembly, or attempts to extract the Service source code, except in cases expressly permitted by mandatory applicable law;
3. introduce or distribute through the Service malware, exploits, phishing links, or other means capable of harming the Service or its Users;
4. disrupt the integrity and stability of the Service operation, including through DDoS, flooding, or intentional load attacks.

### 4.6. Prohibited content

It is prohibited to transmit to the Service, use the Service to analyze or distribute, or create with the help of the Service:

1. materials that exploit minors (Child Sexual Abuse Material, CSAM); upon detection of such content, the Operator deletes it, blocks the Account, and reports the incident to authorized law enforcement authorities and/or specialized organizations in accordance with applicable law;
2. materials that incite hatred and discrimination on the basis of race, nationality, religion, sex, sexual orientation, disability, and other characteristics protected by applicable law;
3. materials that call for violence, terrorist or extremist activity, or support terrorist organizations;
4. materials that violate sanctions and export control regimes;
5. materials related to illegal trafficking of narcotic substances, weapons, explosives, and other substances and items prohibited from circulation under applicable law;
6. materials constituting commercial, banking, medical, or other legally protected secrets, where access to such materials was obtained in violation of applicable law;
7. other content recognized as illegal or clearly harmful under applicable law.

## 5. Use of artificial intelligence

When using the AI functionality of the Service, the User undertakes to:

1. take into account the AI limitations set out in the AI Disclaimer, including the possibility of errors, “hallucinations”, and model bias;
2. not use the Service to cause harm to the life, health, or property of third parties;
3. not use the Service in tasks requiring increased accuracy, including medical, legal, financial, and engineering tasks, without independently verifying the Results and involving qualified specialists.

In addition, it is prohibited to:

1. use the Service to create deepfake content, fake voice or video messages, or imitation of the speech or style of specific persons for the purpose of misleading or causing harm;
2. use the Service to impersonate another individual or legal entity, including by imitating speech style or falsifying correspondence context;
3. attempt to extract system prompts, bypass instructions (jailbreak), inject hidden instructions (prompt injection), or otherwise manipulate AI models in a manner that disrupts the operation of the Service or AI providers;
4. use the Service for automated classification or assessment of individuals with legal or other similarly significant effects on them without an appropriate legal basis.

## 6. Subscription restrictions

When using the Subscriptions feature, the User undertakes to:

1. not use Subscriptions for mass or automated monitoring that violates the rules of source platforms, primarily Telegram;
2. not use Subscriptions to circumvent restrictions imposed on the User or on the analyzed Telegram sources;
3. observe reasonable limits of functionality use; not create Subscriptions solely for the purpose of circumventing Plan limits or emulating a higher Plan.

The Service may suspend or delete Subscriptions that violate this Policy in the manner provided in the “Violations and consequences” section.

## 7. Violations and consequences

If a violation of this Policy is identified, if there are reasonable suspicions of a violation, or if third-party complaints are received, the Service may apply one or more of the following measures:

1. send the User a warning describing the identified violation and requiring its removal;
2. temporarily restrict access to individual Service functions, in particular creation of Subscriptions, sending notifications, or use of AI functionality;
3. suspend or delete individual Subscriptions;
4. temporarily or permanently block the User’s Account;
5. terminate the provision of services under the Terms of Service.

The decision to apply measures is made by the Operator at its sole discretion, taking into account the nature of the violation, its recurrence, potential harm, and other relevant circumstances. The Operator strives to apply measures proportionately to the nature and severity of the violation.

When applying measures related to access restrictions, the Operator strives to notify the User at the email address specified in the Account, indicating:

1. the nature of the identified violation and the measure applied;
2. the legal or factual basis for applying the measure;
3. the appeal procedure provided in the “Appealing decisions and internal complaint handling procedure” section.

In cases where prior notification is impossible or may harm the security of the Service, its Users, or third parties, in particular when CSAM is detected, during an active cyberattack, or upon requirements of authorized authorities, notification is sent simultaneously with the application of the measure or within a reasonable time after its application.

## 8. Appealing decisions and internal complaint handling procedure

The User has the right to appeal a decision applied to them regarding access restriction, deletion of Subscriptions, or Account blocking within 30 (thirty) calendar days from the date of receiving notice of the measure, or from the date when the User became or should have become aware of its application.

The complaint must be sent by email to [anastasiya.polka@gmail.com](mailto:anastasiya.polka@gmail.com) with the subject line “AUP Appeal” and must contain:

1. Account identifier, meaning the registration email;
2. description of the challenged measure and the date of its application;
3. justification of the User’s position and, where necessary, supporting materials.

The Operator reviews the complaint within a reasonable time, but no later than 30 (thirty) calendar days from receipt, and sends the User a reasoned response. If, following review of the complaint, the decision is revised in favor of the User, access to the Service is restored in whole or in part; if the decision is upheld, the User is informed of the reasons.

The provisions of this section apply taking into account the requirements of applicable digital services legislation, including the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act, DSA) for internal complaint handling systems, to the extent that such legislation applies to the Service and the User.

This procedure does not deprive the User of the right to contact competent public authorities or a court in accordance with applicable law.

## 10. Vulnerability Disclosure

The Operator welcomes responsible disclosure of information about Service vulnerabilities. If a technical vulnerability is discovered, including security vulnerabilities, authentication errors, or data leaks, the researcher or other person acting in good faith is encouraged to send a report by email to [anastasiya.polka@gmail.com](mailto:anastasiya.polka@gmail.com) with the subject line “Security Report”.

It is desirable to include in the report:

1. a description of the discovered vulnerability and its potential impact;
2. reproduction steps sufficient to confirm the vulnerability (proof of concept);
3. the environment used and the time of discovery;
4. the researcher’s contact details, at the researcher’s discretion.

When making a responsible disclosure, the researcher undertakes to:

1. act in good faith and not use the discovered vulnerability for purposes beyond its confirmation;
2. not access data of other Users, and not modify or delete other people’s data beyond the minimum necessary to confirm the vulnerability;
3. provide the Operator with a reasonable period, not less than 30 calendar days, to remediate the vulnerability before public disclosure.

If these conditions are observed, the Operator does not apply to the researcher the measures provided in the “Violations and consequences” section and strives to cooperate with the researcher in the manner agreed by the parties. The provisions of this section do not grant the researcher any right to compensation or reward unless expressly agreed with the Operator.
