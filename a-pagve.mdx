## 6. Automatic renewal (Auto-Renewal)

The subscription renews automatically for the next billing period unless the User cancels it in advance. Funds are charged from the linked payment method at the beginning of the new billing period at the price effective at that time.

By subscribing to a paid Plan, the User expressly agrees to automatic renewal and to the corresponding amounts being charged from their payment method until the subscription is cancelled.

Notifications related to automatic renewal are sent to the email address specified in the Account and/or displayed in the Service interface. In particular:

1. for monthly subscriptions, the User receives a receipt after each successful charge;
2. for subscriptions with a period significantly longer than one month, if such subscriptions are introduced, notice of the upcoming automatic renewal and charge amount is sent no later than 7 (seven) calendar days before the charge date, unless applicable law establishes another period;
3. when the subscription price changes, notice is sent in the manner provided in the “Changes to prices and terms” section.

The User may cancel automatic renewal at any time in the Account settings or, if necessary, in the payment provider’s interface. Cancellation instructions are published in the Service and may be requested by email from the Operator.

For Users in the State of California, USA, and other jurisdictions where special requirements for automatically renewing subscriptions apply, including the California Auto-Renewal Law / CARL, the Operator ensures explicit User consent to auto-renewal, sending of email confirmation, and the availability of a clear subscription cancellation mechanism in the Service.

## 7. Subscription cancellation

The User may cancel the subscription at any time through the Service interface. After cancellation:

1. access to paid features is retained until the end of the paid period;
2. no further charges are made;
3. at the end of the paid period, the Account is switched to the Free Plan unless the User has selected otherwise.

Subscription cancellation does not mean automatic deletion of the Account. Account deletion is performed separately in the manner described in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Refunds are made through the same payment provider and by the same method used for payment. The timing of refund crediting is determined by the provider and the User’s bank.

1. the subscription is considered suspended;
2. the Account is switched to the Free Plan;
3. active Subscriptions exceeding Free limits are automatically suspended;
4. previously saved data is not deleted and becomes fully available again when payment is resumed, except where deletion is provided by other Service policies.

The User may resume the subscription at any time by updating payment method details in the Service interface or with the payment provider.
