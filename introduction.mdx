## What CoTel does

CoTel reads messages from the Telegram account you authorize, sends them to an AI model for processing, and returns a structured result. You can run a one-time analysis on demand or set up automatic subscriptions that monitor a chat, channel, or group on a schedule and send you results through notifications from the CoTel Telegram bot.

If you do not want to authorize your personal Telegram account in our service, you can use CoTel service accounts, which allow you to use the service with public channels and groups.

The interface is available in **English** and **Russian**, and you can switch languages at any time using the globe icon in the navigation panel.

## Chat analysis on demand

Formulate a question for a specific Telegram chat, channel, or group — CoTel will access the history of the selected chat, find what matters, prepare a summary of the discussion, and return a structured answer. Examples of how this feature can be used:

| Type | What it produces |
| --- | --- |
| **Finding a specific discussion** | For questions like “Was the launch of the new feature discussed in the chat?”. CoTel searches relevant messages in the history, finds the needed part of the discussion, and returns a short answer with context. |
| **Getting the main topics for a period** | Ask: “What were the main topics discussed over the last 7 days?”. CoTel analyzes messages for the selected period and highlights the main topics, recurring questions, important conclusions, and discussion dynamics. |
| **News summary from a channel** | Select a news or industry Telegram channel and ask: “Summarize today’s news”. CoTel collects the key news, removes noise, and returns a compact digest. |
| **Extracting ideas from a discussion** | Example query: “What ideas were suggested in the chat?”. CoTel collects suggestions, hypotheses, product thoughts, marketing ideas, or possible solutions. |
| **Finding decisions made** | For the question “What decisions were made this week?”, CoTel separates decisions from the general discussion and returns a list of agreements, selected options, and next steps. |

There are many more ways to use Telegram message history analysis — it all depends on your goals and work context. You can learn more about use cases **here**.

## Automatic subscriptions

Subscriptions allow CoTel to periodically monitor a chat or channel on your behalf. You define a topic or prompt, choose how often the history should be checked, and CoTel runs the analysis automatically — then sends you a Telegram notification when it finds relevant content or when a new digest is ready. The Free plan includes up to 2 trial subscriptions for 5 days; paid plans unlock up to 20 active subscriptions with more frequent checks.

## AI models

Two AI models are currently available for analysis:

- **OpenAI GPT-4.1 mini** — fast general-purpose analysis, used by default on the Free plan
- **Claude Sonnet 4.6** — deeper analysis suitable for long discussions, available on paid plans
