CoTel is an AI-powered service that lets you analyze any Telegram chat, channel, or group without leaving your browser. Instead of scrolling through thousands of messages, you describe what you need and CoTel's AI does the reading for you — returning structured summaries, topic digests, smart search results, or extracted keywords in seconds.

## What CoTel does

CoTel reads messages from a Telegram source you authorize, sends them to an AI model for processing, and returns a structured result. You can run one-off analyses on demand or set up automated subscriptions that monitor a chat on a schedule and push findings to you via a Telegram bot notification.

The interface is available in **English** and **Russian**, switchable at any time via the globe icon in the navigation bar.

## Analysis types

CoTel offers four core analysis modes:

| Type | What it produces |
|---|---|
| **Summarization** | A concise summary of all messages in the selected period, highlighting key ideas and conclusions |
| **Notes / Digest** | Structured notes organized by topic and subtopic — useful for long technical or project discussions |
| **Search** | AI-powered semantic search across chat history, returning relevant messages with context and source references |
| **Keywords** | Automatic extraction of important terms, names, entities, and topics from the conversation |

## Automated subscriptions

Subscriptions let CoTel periodically monitor a chat or channel on your behalf. You define a topic or prompt, choose a check frequency, and CoTel runs the analysis automatically — then sends you a Telegram notification when it finds relevant content or has a new digest ready. The Free plan includes up to 2 trial subscriptions; paid plans unlock up to 20 active subscriptions with higher check frequencies.

## AI models

Two AI models are available for analysis:

- **OpenAI GPT-4.1 mini** — fast, general-purpose analysis (default on the Free plan)
- **Claude Sonnet 4.6** — deeper analysis suited to long discussions (available on paid plans)
