Each CoTel plan includes limits on daily and monthly requests, the depth of history available for analysis, the number of subscriptions, and how often they can run. Understanding these limits helps you avoid interruptions and choose the right plan for your workload.

<Tip>
  Before creating subscriptions, check the **Limits** tab in your profile — it shows exactly how much usage remains on your current plan.
</Tip>

## Checking current usage

Open your profile by clicking the avatar in the sidebar, then select the **Limits** tab. You will see:

- **Requests today** — how many requests you have used today out of your daily quota
- **Requests this month** — how many requests you have used this month out of your monthly quota
- **Active subscriptions** — how many subscriptions are currently active. Subscriptions can exist without being active. If a subscription is paused, it is not counted toward the active subscription limit.
- **History analysis depth** — the maximum history window available for Telegram chat requests, for example “up to 7 days”.
- **Minimum subscription frequency** — the shortest interval between subscription checks allowed on your plan. In practice, this is the subscription run frequency.
- **Chat and channel history** — whether saved chat and channel history is available on your plan. This feature lets you see a list of recent chats you have queried, so you can quickly select them again.
- **Trial subscriptions** — how many trial subscriptions have already been used, only for the Free plan.

## What happens when you reach a limit

When you exceed a limit, CoTel shows an error message. Below is what each message means and how to fix it.

**Daily request limit reached**\
You have used all daily requests. The quota resets at the beginning of the next day. Upgrade to a higher plan to increase your daily request quota.

**Monthly request limit reached**\
You have used all monthly requests. The quota resets at the beginning of the next month. Consider switching to a plan with a higher monthly quota.

**History limit exceeded**\
Your plan allows analysis of no more than X days of history. Reduce the history period in **Query settings** or switch to a plan with deeper history access.

**Active subscription limit reached**\
You have reached the active subscription limit for your plan. Pause one of your existing subscriptions to free up space, or switch to a plan that allows more active subscriptions.

**Frequency too high**\
Your plan does not allow the selected check frequency. The minimum allowed interval for your current plan is shown in the error message. Choose a less frequent interval when creating or editing the subscription.

**Trial access to subscriptions already used**\
Trial access to subscriptions has already been used. After reaching this limit on the Free plan, you cannot create additional trial subscriptions. Upgrade to a paid plan to create new subscriptions.

**Trial subscriptions have ended**\
All your trial subscriptions have expired. Upgrade to a paid plan to continue using subscriptions.

## Trial subscriptions

The Free plan includes up to 2 trial subscriptions so you can try the subscription feature before switching to a paid plan.

- Trial subscriptions expire automatically after the trial period ends. During the MVP stage, the trial period is 5 days.
- Trial subscriptions cannot be deleted manually — they remain visible until they expire.
- After trial access has been used, when the limit is reached or all trial subscriptions have expired, you need to switch to a paid plan to create new subscriptions.

To continue using subscriptions after the trial period ends, switch to the Basic or Pro plan from your profile. See [Pricing](/plans/overview) for the full comparison.
