Every CoTel plan comes with limits on daily and monthly requests, history depth, subscription count, and how often subscriptions can run. Understanding these limits helps you avoid interruptions and choose the right plan for your workload.

<Tip>
  Check the Limits tab in your profile before creating subscriptions — it shows exactly how much capacity you have remaining on your current plan.
</Tip>

## Checking your current usage

Open your profile by clicking your avatar in the sidebar, then select the **Limits** tab. You will see:

- **Requests today** — how many requests you have used today out of your daily quota
- **Requests this month** — how many requests you have used this month out of your monthly quota
- **Active subscriptions** — how many subscriptions are currently active
- **History analysis depth** — the maximum history window available to you (e.g., "up to 7 days")
- **Minimum subscription frequency** — the shortest interval allowed between subscription checks on your plan
- **Chat and channel history** — whether access to saved chat and channel history is available on your plan
- **Trial subscriptions** — how many trial subscriptions have been used (Free plan only)

## What happens when you hit a limit

When you exceed a limit, CoTel shows an error message. Here is what each one means and how to resolve it.

**Daily request limit reached**
You have used all of your daily requests. The quota resets at the start of the next day. Upgrade your plan to increase your daily request quota.

**Monthly request limit reached**
You have used all of your monthly requests. The quota resets at the start of the next month. Consider upgrading to a plan with a higher monthly quota.

**History limit exceeded**
Your plan allows analyzing no more than X days of history. Reduce the history period in Query settings, or upgrade to a plan with a deeper history window.

**Active subscriptions limit reached**
You have reached the active subscriptions limit for your plan. Pause an existing subscription to free up a slot, or upgrade to a plan that allows more active subscriptions.

**Frequency too high**
Your plan does not allow the selected check frequency. The minimum allowed interval on your current plan is shown in the error message. Select a less frequent interval when creating or editing a subscription.

**Trial subscription access already used**
Trial subscription access has already been used. You cannot create additional trial subscriptions on the Free plan once this limit is reached. Upgrade to a paid plan to create new subscriptions.

**Trial subscriptions ended**
All of your trial subscriptions have expired. Upgrade to a paid plan to continue using subscriptions.

## Trial subscriptions

The Free plan includes up to 2 trial subscriptions to let you experience the subscriptions feature before committing to a paid plan.

- Trial subscriptions expire automatically after their trial period ends.
- Trial subscriptions cannot be deleted manually — they remain visible until they expire.
- Once trial access has been used (either the limit is reached or all trials have expired), you must upgrade to a paid plan to create new subscriptions.

To keep using subscriptions after your trials expire, switch to the Basic or Pro plan from your profile. See [Plans](/plans/overview) for a full comparison.
