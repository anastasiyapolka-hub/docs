CoTel offers three plans to match different usage levels. The Free plan lets you try the service at no cost, Basic covers regular daily use, and Pro is built for intensive workloads with the highest request quotas and most active subscriptions.

<Note>
  Pricing may be adjusted after the initial launch once real workload and cost data become available.
</Note>

## Free — $0/month

The Free plan is designed to get you started with the service.

- 3 requests per day / 60 requests per month
- Chat history analysis for queries up to 7 days
- Up to 2 trial subscriptions
- Subscription analysis no more than once every 3 hours
- Telegram notifications

## Basic — $9/month (Recommended)

The Basic plan covers regular use with a generous request quota and a wider history window.

- 20 requests per day / 300 requests per month
- Chat history analysis for queries up to 30 days
- Chat and channel history
- Up to 5 active subscriptions
- Subscription analysis no more than once every hour
- Priority subscription processing
- Telegram notifications

## Pro — $24/month

The Pro plan is built for intensive workloads requiring high request volumes and frequent subscription checks.

- 60 requests per day / 1,500 requests per month
- Chat history analysis for queries up to 90 days
- Chat and channel history
- Up to 20 active subscriptions
- Subscription analysis no more than once every 30 minutes
- Priority subscription processing
- Telegram notifications

## What counts as a request?

A request is one run of chat or channel analysis for a user prompt. Subscriptions are tracked separately and limited by the number of active subscriptions, trial logic, and a minimum check frequency.

## What subscriptions include

A subscription is a periodic AI analysis of new messages in a chosen chat or channel for a given topic, followed by a Telegram notification.

## Switching plans

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your avatar in the sidebar to open your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Limits tab">
    Select the **Limits** tab to review your current usage before switching.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change plan">
    Click the **Change plan** button in your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    Choose the plan you want to switch to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm your selection. Your plan updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
