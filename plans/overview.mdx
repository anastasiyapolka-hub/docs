CoTel offers three plans for different usage scenarios. The Free plan lets you try the service at no cost, Basic is suitable for regular daily use, and Pro is designed for intensive work with the highest request limits and the largest number of active subscriptions.

<Note>
  Plan pricing may be adjusted after the initial launch, once real workload and cost data become available.
</Note>

## Free — \$0/month

The Free plan is designed for getting started with the service.

- 3 requests per day / 60 requests per month
- Chat history analysis for requests up to 7 days
- Up to 2 trial subscriptions
- Subscription runs no more than once every 3 hours
- Telegram notifications for subscriptions

## Basic — \$9/month (Recommended)

The Basic plan is suitable for regular use, with an expanded request limit and a wider history window.

- 20 requests per day / 300 requests per month
- Chat history analysis for requests up to 30 days
- Chat and channel history
- Up to 5 active subscriptions
- Subscription analysis no more than once every hour
- Priority subscription processing
- Telegram notifications for subscriptions

## Pro — \$24/month

The Pro plan is designed for intensive work where high request limits and frequent subscription checks are needed.

- 60 requests per day / 1,500 requests per month
- Chat history analysis for requests up to 60 days
- Chat and channel history
- Up to 20 active subscriptions
- Subscription analysis no more than once every 30 minutes
- Priority subscription processing
- Telegram notifications for subscriptions

## What counts as a request?

A request is one run of chat, group, or channel analysis based on a user prompt. Subscriptions are counted separately and limited by the number of active subscriptions, trial logic, and the minimum check frequency.

## What subscriptions include

A subscription is a periodic AI analysis of new messages in a selected chat or channel on a given topic, followed by a Telegram notification from the CoTel bot.

## Changing your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your avatar in the sidebar to open your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Limits tab">
    Select the **Limits** tab to view your current usage before changing your plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change plan">
    Click the **Change plan** button in your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Choose the plan you want to switch to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm your choice. Your plan will be updated immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
