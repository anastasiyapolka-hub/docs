## 2. Principles for selecting sub-processors

When selecting sub-processors, the Operator follows these principles:

1. the sub-processor has appropriate organizational and technical data protection measures;
2. there are legal grounds for cross-border transfer of personal data, including Standard Contractual Clauses or other applicable mechanisms;
3. the sub-processor has public privacy policies and data processing terms available for Users to review;
4. the amount of data transferred is limited to what is strictly necessary in accordance with the data minimization principle.

## 3. Notifications of changes

The Operator notifies Users of changes to the composition of sub-processors by:

1. updating this List with the effective date of the changes;
2. displaying a prominent notice in the Service interface in the event of material changes;
3. sending an email notification if the change affects the categories of transferred data or processing jurisdictions.

A User who does not agree with the engagement of a new sub-processor may stop using the Service and delete their Account in the manner provided in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
