Subscriptions let CoTel monitor a Telegram chat or channel on your behalf and notify you automatically. Instead of running a manual analysis every time, you configure a subscription once and CoTel checks for new messages at a regular interval, analyzes them with AI, and sends you the results as a message in Telegram.

## What is a subscription?

A subscription is a periodic AI analysis of new messages in a chosen chat or channel for a given topic. When CoTel finds content that matches your query — or when the digest period elapses — it sends a Telegram notification directly to your Telegram account.

## Subscription types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Events" icon="bell">
    Reacts to announcements or alerts that match your query. CoTel sends you a list of the specific messages that matched.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Digest" icon="file-text">
    AI prepares a summary or digest of the chat for the specified read period and delivers it on schedule.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Events** subscriptions are best when you want to be alerted to specific occurrences — for example, a price announcement in a trading channel or a job posting in a community group. CoTel reads new messages, checks whether any match your query, and sends the matching messages to you.

**Digest** subscriptions are best when you want a regular summary of what has been discussed in a chat over the past period, regardless of any specific trigger.

## Creating a subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subscriptions panel">
    In the workspace, expand the **Subscriptions** section in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new subscription">
    Click the **+** button to open the subscription creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a subscription name">
    Type a descriptive name so you can identify the subscription in your list later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a subscription type">
    Choose **Events** or **Digest** depending on what you want CoTel to do. Events sends you matching messages; Digest sends a periodic AI summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the chat or channel">
    Pick a chat from your list or paste a Telegram link directly into the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the read period">
    Choose how often CoTel should check for new messages: every 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, 6 hours, 12 hours, or once a day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an AI model">
    Select the AI model to use for this subscription. Free accounts use OpenAI automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the query text">
    Describe what information to collect and when to notify you. You can use keywords or a more detailed question — the analysis is performed by AI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the subscription">
    Click **Create subscription**. CoTel will begin monitoring on the next scheduled interval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing subscriptions

Once a subscription is created, you can manage it from the Subscriptions panel:

- **Pause / Resume**: Temporarily stop a subscription without deleting it, then resume it when needed.
- **Edit**: Update the name, type, chat, period, model, or query text of an existing subscription.
- **Delete**: Remove a subscription permanently. Note that trial subscriptions cannot be deleted manually.
- **Activate from another mode**: If you have subscriptions created under a different mode (personal account vs. CoTel service account), you can activate them in your current mode from the subscription's action menu.

## Plan limits

Your plan determines how many active subscriptions you can have and the minimum allowed check frequency.

| Plan | Active subscriptions | Minimum frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Up to 2 trial subscriptions | Every 3 hours |
| Basic | Up to 5 | Every 1 hour |
| Pro | Up to 20 | Every 30 minutes |

<Warning>
  Trial subscriptions expire and cannot be deleted manually. Upgrade to a paid plan to unlock full subscription features.
</Warning>

## Important notes

<Note>
  Subscribing to private and personal chats is only available when you are signed in with your personal Telegram account. Public channels can be monitored using the CoTel service account.
</Note>
