A subscription is a periodic AI analysis of new messages in a selected chat or channel based on a topic you define. When CoTel finds content that matches your request, or when it is time to send a summary/digest, it sends a Telegram notification from the CoTel bot directly to your Telegram account.

## Subscription types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Events" icon="bell">
    Reacts to announcements or signals that match your request. CoTel sends you a list of specific messages that matched the topic of your request.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Summary" icon="file-text">
    AI prepares a summary or digest of a chat, group, or channel at the selected frequency and sends it to you in Telegram.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Events** are best suited for cases where you want to receive notifications about specific events — for example, a price announcement in a trading channel, a job posting in a community group, or the sale of a specific item in a marketplace group. CoTel reads new messages, checks whether anything matches your request, and sends you the matching messages.

**Summary** subscriptions are best suited for cases where you want to regularly receive a summary of what was discussed in a chat, group, or channel over the previous period, regardless of a specific trigger.

You can learn more about ways to use subscriptions and practical scenarios **here.**

## Creating a subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscriptions panel">
    In the workspace, open the **Subscriptions** section in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new subscription">
    Click the **\+** button to open the subscription creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a subscription name">
    Enter a clear name so you can easily find this subscription later in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the subscription type">
    Choose **Events** or **Summary** depending on what CoTel should do. Events send matching messages, while Summary sends a periodic AI-generated summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a chat or channel">
    Select a chat from the list or paste a Telegram link directly into the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the reading period">
    Choose how often CoTel should check new messages: every 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, 6 hours, 12 hours, or once a day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an AI model">
    Choose the AI model for this subscription. Free accounts automatically use OpenAI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the request text">
    Describe what information should be collected and when you should be notified. Alternatively, describe the details and format for the summary/digest. You can use keywords or a more detailed question — the analysis is performed with AI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the subscription">
    Click **Create subscription**. CoTel will start monitoring at the next scheduled run.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing subscriptions

After creation, you can manage a subscription from the **Subscriptions** panel:

- **Pause / Resume**: temporarily stop a subscription without deleting it, then turn it back on when needed.
- **Edit**: change the name, type, chat, period, model, or request text of an existing subscription.
- **Delete**: permanently delete a subscription. Please note that trial subscriptions cannot be deleted manually.
- **Activate in the current mode**: if you have subscriptions created in another mode — personal account or CoTel service account — you can activate them in the current mode through the subscription action menu. The subscription will then run either through your personal Telegram session or through a CoTel service account, depending on the mode assigned to the subscription.

<Note>
  Subscriptions created in another service mode continue to work when you switch modes. To disable a subscription created in another mode, switch to that mode and pause it using **Pause**. Alternatively, move the subscription to the current mode using **Activate in the current mode**, and then pause it.
</Note>

## Plan limits

Your plan determines how many active subscriptions you can have and the minimum available frequency for checking chat history.

| Plan | Active subscriptions | Minimum frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Up to 2 trial subscriptions | Every 3 hours |
| Basic | Up to 5 | Every 1 hour |
| Pro | Up to 20 | Every 30 minutes |

<Warning>
  Trial subscriptions have a limited duration and cannot be deleted manually — 5 days. Upgrade to a paid plan to unlock the full subscription functionality.
</Warning>

## Important notes

<Note>
  Subscriptions to private and personal chats are available only when you sign in through your personal Telegram account. Public channels can be monitored using a CoTel service account.
</Note>
